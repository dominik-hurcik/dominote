Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

The short version

Hebdo collects zero personal data. There are no user accounts, no analytics, no crash trackers, no ads, and no Hebdo servers.

Where your notes live

Every note you create in Hebdo is a plain Markdown (.md) file stored either locally on your device or in your own private Apple iCloud Drive folder (iCloud Drive / Hebdo) if you choose to enable iCloud sync.

Your notes are never transmitted to or stored by the developer of Hebdo. When iCloud Drive is enabled, syncing is handled directly by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy.

Third-party services

Hebdo for macOS and Hebdo for iOS do not include third-party analytics SDKs, advertising frameworks, or external tracking services.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, you can open an issue on github.com/dominik-hurcik/hebdo.
